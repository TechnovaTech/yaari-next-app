# Truecaller Login - User Guide

## If Truecaller Shows Error

### The app will automatically use OTP instead ✅

When you click "Continue with Truecaller" and see an error, the app automatically switches to OTP login. Just enter your phone number and OTP.

## Why Truecaller Might Not Work

1. **Network/WiFi Issue** - Your network blocks Truecaller
2. **Truecaller Not Installed** - Install Truecaller app
3. **DNS Issue** - Your ISP/WiFi cannot reach Truecaller servers

## Solution: Use OTP Login

**OTP login always works and is recommended:**

1. Enter your 10-digit phone number
2. Click "Get OTP"
3. Enter the OTP you receive
4. Login successfully ✅

## No Action Needed

The app handles everything automatically. If Truecaller fails, it switches to OTP instantly.
